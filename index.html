Questions

Before you climb

Ladder is a decentralized exchange on Elysium. It combines a classic AMM for instant swaps and liquidity pools with a memecoin launchpad and ladder-style limit orders — one order spread across several price levels, filling step by step. The rollout is phased: swaps, liquidity and the memecoin launchpad first; ladder orders follow in Phase 02.

On Elysium, Kinetiq's Arbitrum Orbit L2 for the Hyperliquid ecosystem. Ladder runs on Elysium Testnet today (chain ID 99801, gas token HYPE) and moves to mainnet when Elysium mainnet launches on 9 November 2026.

Use the official Elysium faucet. It requires the claiming wallet to hold a balance on HyperEVM or Hyperliquid mainnet. You can also bridge HYPE from HyperEVM testnet, where it converts 1:1.

Every swap pays a 0.3% fee. That fee stays in the pool and is shared by liquidity providers in proportion to their LP share, so a position grows with trading volume. Liquidity can be withdrawn at any time.

At mainnet, yes — through routing, not by moving liquidity. Ladder Earn's aggregator framework registers other Elysium venues once their routers are public, compares quotes across them, and routes each trade to the deepest pool. Liquidity always stays in its home pool; Ladder is the doorway.

No and no. Ladder currently runs on testnet only, where tokens have no monetary value, and the contracts have not been audited. Use a wallet that holds nothing of value, and never treat testnet activity as financial advice.
